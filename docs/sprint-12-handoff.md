# Sprint 12 Handoff

Branch: `feature/sprint-12-tournament-engine`

This sprint adds the Tournament Engine foundation for UR Series, UR Cup and UR Legends. The implementation is additive and preserves UR Play.

Delivered:

- tournament domain model migration
- multi-set tournament rules
- format selector
- eligibility service rules
- Series to Cup qualification rules
- Legends doubles/fours seed helpers
- UR Play rotation rest rule helper
- scheduled rounds helper
- admin tournament pages
- operator tournament page
- athlete competitions pages
- public competitions pages
- unit and integration coverage for core invariants

Explicitly out of scope:

- UR Coins
- UR Market
- online payments
- repasses
- sponsors
- AI
- video automation
- external push

Sprint 13 must not start automatically.
